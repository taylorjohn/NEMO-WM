## Methodology

Each capability has two independent properties:

- **GROUNDED**: has a citation linking it to a known mechanism in cognitive science / neuroscience.
- **TESTED**: has executable code that runs and asserts (not just 'function exists').

Status is the cross-product. The strongest claim NeMo-WM can make is **PRODUCTION** — both grounded AND tested.
